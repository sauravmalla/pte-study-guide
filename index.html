Speaking Fundamentals
Getting 90 is a MUST — Focus on these two pillars
⚡ Key Rule: Speaking is marked on Content, Oral Fluency, and Pronunciation. Oral Fluency is the most critical — speaking fluently should be prioritized over Content and Pronunciation.
🌊 A. Oral Fluency Most Important
  • Speak continuously without long pauses — flow matters more than perfection.
  • Avoid filler words like "um", "uh", "like" — they break your fluency score.
  • Maintain a natural rhythm — not too fast, not too slow.
🗣️ B. Pronunciation → Clarity Important
  • Pronounce words clearly — clarity is scored, not accent.
  • Focus on word stress and intonation patterns.
📖 Read Aloud Task Type
✅ STUDIED
Read AS IT IS — No Addition, Omission, or Repetition.
🔁 Repeat Sentence Task Type
✅ STUDIED
Write the initial of the first half and speak fluently and clearly. Use the 3-SECOND rule to capture and recall.
Describe Image
Universal formats for any image type
💡 Checklist: 1) Picture identity / Title  2) Quality  3) X/Y-axis  4) Min/Max value  5) Other content  6) Colour / Shape  7) Conclusion
🌐 Universal Format (General Images)
✅ STUDIED

1. The given image / line graph / bar graph / pie chart that represents TITLE.

2. This image has various details to analyze.

3. The image includes various shapes, sizes, colors, and details.

4. It displays a range of elements, such as element 1 and element 2.

5. In the image, key details like specific content 1 and specific content 2 can be noted.

6. Additionally, it highlights another prominent feature or detail.

7. Overall, the content in this image is important for understanding TOPIC.

📊 For Graphs (Bar / Line / Scatter)
✅ STUDIED

1. A Line/Bar/Mix graph can be seen that represents Title + trend or comparison.

2. This graph has a very clear layout, which makes it easy to analyze.

3. The graph includes various shapes, lines, and colors.

4. The lines are marked with different values, ranging from low value to high value.

5. There are colors like blue, green, and red, with blue being my favorite color.

6. Additionally, the graph shows data on Content 1 and Content 2.

7. It also shows another key data point or comparison.

8. Overall, the information shown in this graph is crucial for understanding main Title/Content.

📷 For Photographs (Scenic / People / Objects)
✅ STUDIED

1. The photograph shows description of subject/scene/title.

2. It is a visually appealing image that captures attention.

3. The photo includes a mix of different shapes, sizes, and colors.

4. There are a variety of details, with highlights such as element 1 and element 2.

6. In the photograph, specific detail 1 and specific detail 2 can be seen.

Re-tell Lecture
Listen → Write 10 keywords → Speak from template
📝 Step 1: Listen to the audio and write minimum 10 main keywords while listening.
📋 Basic Re-tell Template Core Format
✅ STUDIED

1. The lecture focused on main topic and covered several key areas related to it.

2. Initially, the speaker introduced point 1, followed by a discussion on point 2 and point 3.

3. The speaker then expanded on point 4, explaining its relevance and connection to related idea or detail.

4. Additionally, point 5 and point 6 were discussed, providing further depth to the main topic.

5. In the latter part of the lecture, he/she mentioned additional detail or example to illustrate another aspect or importance.

6. Towards the end, the speaker summarized with a key takeaway, emphasizing final insight or message.

7. Overall, the lecture offered valuable information and insights on the topic.

⭐ Format 2 — Intermediate English → USE THIS
✅ STUDIED

1. The lecture was mainly focused on topic and related topic.

2. Initially, the speaker introduced point 1, followed by explanations on point 2 and point 3.

3. Secondly, he moved on to discuss point 4, explaining how it relates to point 5 and point 6.

4. Later, he elaborated on point 7, mentioning examples such as example 1 and example 2.

5. Furthermore, the speaker highlighted additional detail and its importance in relevant context.

6. He also explored additional topic to provide more depth.

7. In the final part of the lecture, he summed up with key insights on summary point.

8. In conclusion, this lecture presented valuable insights and detailed information on the subject.

🔥 Format 3 — Advanced English → USE THIS
✅ STUDIED

1. The lecture primarily explored the topics of main theme and secondary theme, delving into their significance.

2. The speaker began by examining key aspect 1 and proceeded to discuss key aspect 2 and key aspect 3 in considerable detail.

3. Subsequently, he analyzed concept 1, linking it to concept 2 and concept 3 to enhance understanding.

4. In addition, he provided an in-depth explanation of insight 1 and insight 2, supported by relevant examples such as example.

5. Further into the lecture, the speaker underscored the importance of important detail within the broader context.

6. He also addressed additional topic, emphasizing its implications and broader impact.

7. Towards the end, he synthesized the information with concluding points on summary insight.

8. Overall, this lecture was highly informative, offering a comprehensive analysis and insightful perspectives on the topic.

Summarize Group Discussion
Listen, divide into 3 speakers, note main ideas
📌 Steps to Follow Process
  • Listen — divide into 3 Speakers using the given spaces.
  • Note down the main idea of each speaker (not only words!).
  • Remember the accent of each speaker — they can talk throughout the audio.
  • Highlight the main keywords you will use.
🔥 Hard Level — Advanced/Fluent English → TRY TO USE THIS!
✅ STUDIED

The discussion primarily focused around main topic.

The first speaker elaborated on point 1, and further reflected on point 2, highlighting how point 3 plays a key role in the overall experience.

Subsequently, the second speaker compared it with point 1, providing insight into point 2, and emphasizing the importance of point 3.

The third speaker shared personal views on point 1, offering specific examples about point 2, and concluded with thoughts on point 3.

As the conversation progressed, it ended by stressing conclusion idea. Overall, the discussion presented diverse perspectives, particularly focusing on main takeaway 1 and main takeaway 2.

Respond to a Situation
You can see the written prompt — no need to listen to audio
📌 Steps Process
  • Write down the Main Problem.
  • Write down the solution.
  • Offer 2 Suggestions!!
  • Conclude with a closing statement.
📋 1. Formal Template
✅ STUDIED

Greeting: Dear Name/Sir/Madam,

I understand that briefly state the problem.

I would like to recommend main solution, as this will reason or benefit.

In addition, you may consider extra suggestion 1 and extra suggestion 2 to improve the situation.

If required, I am available to personal offer of assistance.

I trust these suggestions will help resolve the matter effectively. Thank you.

Summarise Spoken Text
50–70 words | 10 minutes per question
📌 Steps Process
  • Listen to the audio, understand it, and write keywords at the same time.
  • Type the format first AS IT IS.
  • Replace Keywords with your content.
  • Proof Read before submitting.
✍️ Primary Format Template IDEAL: 60–65 words
SOMETHING = KEYWORD  |  Do + ing = e.g. getting, improving
✅ STUDIED

The talk delineates the importance of SOMETHING in doing SOMETHING ELSE. According to the speaker, it is by doing SOMETHING not only helps in doing TASK 1, but also helps in doing TASK 2. Hence, the role of SOMETHING in doing SOMETHING ELSE is instrumental indeed.

Write Essay
200–300 words | 20 minutes | Discuss both views + opinion
⚡ Key Facts: Essay is scored on Content, Development & Structure, Grammar, Vocabulary, Spelling. Aim for 250–270 words — under 200 loses marks automatically.
🏆 Universal Essay Template 79+ Target
📌 Introduction
✅ STUDIED

1. In today's rapidly changing world, the issue of TOPIC has attracted significant attention.

2. While some people believe that VIEW 1, others argue that VIEW 2.

3. This essay will discuss both perspectives before presenting a reasoned conclusion.

📌 Body Paragraph 1 — Advantages
✅ STUDIED

1. On the one hand, there are several advantages associated with TOPIC.

2. Firstly, it can contribute to individual and societal development.

3. Secondly, it often improves efficiency, productivity, and overall quality of life.

4. For example, many countries and organizations have successfully implemented such approaches and achieved positive outcomes.

5. Therefore, these benefits cannot be overlooked.

📌 Body Paragraph 2 — Disadvantages
✅ STUDIED

1. On the other hand, certain drawbacks should also be considered.

2. One major concern is that it may create social, economic, or environmental challenges.

3. Furthermore, improper implementation can lead to unintended negative consequences.

4. For instance, various sectors have experienced difficulties when adequate planning and regulation were absent.

5. Consequently, these issues deserve careful attention.

📌 Conclusion
✅ STUDIED

1. In conclusion, although TOPIC presents some disadvantages, its benefits are generally more significant when managed effectively.

2. In my opinion, a balanced approach is essential to maximize advantages while minimizing potential drawbacks.

Reading Templates
📖 Reading Logic
  • Reading Fill in the Blanks: Focus on grammar, collocations, and vocabulary.
  • Re-order Paragraphs: Find the topic sentence first, then link pronouns and connectors.
  • Multiple Choice: Read the question first, then scan for evidence.
  • Reading & Writing FIB: Check grammar, tense, article, and preposition usage.
Listening Templates
🎧 Listening Logic
  • Summarize Spoken Text: Write keywords while listening and use a fixed template.
  • Listening Fill in the Blanks: Predict grammar and spelling before the audio reaches the gap.
  • Highlight Incorrect Words: Follow the transcript carefully and mark mismatches immediately.
  • Write From Dictation: Prioritize spelling, word order, and sentence accuracy.
