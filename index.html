Speaking Fundamentals
Getting 90 is a MUST — Focus on these two pillars
⚡ Key Rule: Speaking is marked on Content, Oral Fluency, and Pronunciation. Oral Fluency is the most critical — speaking fluently should be prioritized over Content and Pronunciation.
🌊 A. Oral Fluency Most Important
  • Speak continuously without long pauses — flow matters more than perfection.
  • Avoid filler words like "um", "uh", "like" — they break your fluency score.
  • Maintain a natural rhythm — not too fast, not too slow.
🗣️ B. Pronunciation → Clarity Important
  • Pronounce words clearly — clarity is scored, not accent.
  • Focus on word stress and intonation patterns.

Summarize Group Discussion
Listen, divide into 3 speakers, note main ideas
📌 Steps to Follow Process
  • Listen — divide into 3 Speakers using the given spaces.
  • Note down the main idea of each speaker (not only words!).
  • Remember the accent of each speaker — they can talk throughout the audio.
  • Highlight the main keywords you will use.

⚠️ Important (Aug 2025 update): Human reviewers now check essay content for originality. Generic fillers like "societal development" and "quality of life" without specific examples will lower your content score.
📌 Introduction
✅ STUDIED

1. In today's rapidly changing world, the issue of TOPIC has attracted significant attention.

2. While some people believe that VIEW 1, others argue that VIEW 2.

3. This essay will discuss both perspectives before presenting a reasoned conclusion.

📌 Body Paragraph 1 — Advantages / First View
✅ STUDIED

1. On the one hand, there are several advantages associated with TOPIC.

2. Firstly, it can contribute to individual and societal development.

3. Secondly, it often improves efficiency, productivity, and overall quality of life.

4. For example, many countries and organizations have successfully implemented such approaches and achieved positive outcomes.

5. Therefore, these benefits cannot be overlooked.

📄 Full Essay — Assembled View Read as one piece
✅ STUDIED

— INTRODUCTION —

In today's rapidly changing world, the issue of TOPIC has attracted significant attention. While some people believe that VIEW 1, others argue that VIEW 2. This essay will discuss both perspectives before presenting a reasoned conclusion.


— BODY PARAGRAPH 1 —

On the one hand, there are several advantages associated with TOPIC. Firstly, it can contribute to individual and societal development. Secondly, it often improves efficiency, productivity, and overall quality of life. For example, many countries and organizations have successfully implemented such approaches and achieved positive outcomes. Therefore, these benefits cannot be overlooked.


— BODY PARAGRAPH 2 —

On the other hand, certain drawbacks should also be considered. One major concern is that it may create social, economic, or environmental challenges. Furthermore, improper implementation can lead to unintended negative consequences. For instance, various sectors have experienced difficulties when adequate planning and regulation were absent. Consequently, these issues deserve careful attention.


— CONCLUSION —

In conclusion, although TOPIC presents some disadvantages, its benefits are generally more significant when managed effectively. In my opinion, a balanced approach is essential to maximize advantages while minimizing potential drawbacks.

💡 Essay Writing Tips Score Boosters
  • Word count: Stay between 250–270 words. Under 200 = automatic mark deduction. Over 300 = no bonus, just more chances for errors.
  • Specific examples: Replace "many countries" with a real one: "countries like Finland", "sectors such as healthcare". Specificity raises your content score from 3 to 5+.
  • Vary sentence starters: Don't begin 3 sentences with "It". Use "This", "Such", "These benefits", "The implementation of..."
  • Spelling is 6 marks: Proof read in the last 2 minutes. The AI is strict — "goverment", "enviroment" are common costly errors.
  • Connectors to vary: Besides "Furthermore" and "Consequently", also use: "Moreover", "In contrast", "Nevertheless", "As a result", "Despite this".
  • Never leave a body paragraph with only generic examples. "Various sectors" is not an example — it's a placeholder. Name at least one real context per paragraph.
